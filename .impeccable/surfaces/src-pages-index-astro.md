# Homepage (and site-wide world): evolved neo-brutalism

Scope: the whole site's visual world (tokens, type, components) with the homepage as the lead surface. Visitor mode: Persuade (homepage); Read (posts, blog index); Operate-light (CV page, print and PDF must keep working).

Audience and job: recruiters, hiring managers and founders deciding in under a minute whether to talk to Teodor; engineers arriving on posts. Action: download the CV or email. Proof: career track tenure, Deel figures in the proof line, featured QA-context build with its diagram, post result numbers.

Constraints: no-JS rendering, WCAG AA in both themes, 320px without overflow, CV print stylesheet and PDF, stable URLs, the four brights stay defined because post SVG diagrams use them.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A practitioner's rack, not a template kit. Refuses the generic neo-brutalist starter (four rotating brights, dot grid, Inter + Space Grotesk) and the standard avatar-name-icons hero.

OWN-WORLD: Cream paper, near-black ink, 3px outlines, hard offset shadows, press-in buttons. Two colours with meaning only: signal yellow = applied AI, electric blue = homelab (accent only: labels, outlines, shadows, never full fills). Section headings are 1U rack faceplates with screws and a vent grille; on screens 1340px and up the page hangs between two rack rails with real 1U hole spacing. Bricolage Grotesque heavy condensed display, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next body, Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for data.

STORY: The visitor sees a senior applied AI engineer with receipts: who he is, a proof line, tenure on a rack-mounted career track, and a featured AI build; then downloads the CV or emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Huge two-line name at display scale left, taped photo print right, AI-yellow role sticker, pitch, proof line, primary yellow "Download CV (PDF)" and "Email me" at 56px, copyable address; career faceplate begins at the fold on desktop.

FORM: User-pinned direction "Evolved neo-brutalism", mockup 6 of six rendered in the 2026-10-09 exploration. No concept-seed roll ran; provenance is the user's own pick after reviewing all six rendered mockups side by side (their words: "These seem fine I will think about them", then "do the evolved neo"). Seed key: user-pick:mock-6-evolved. Build decisions confirmed by the user: whole site in scope, faceplates plus rails, blue as an accent only. Signature interaction: the career track (playhead entrance, pick a role to reveal highlights).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
